Swedish pop star Zara Larsson became the latest celebrity to join Portland’s bike bus movement when she took a joyride through Northeast Portland this weekend.

> “Portland bike bus I love youuuuu,” Larsson [posted on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@zaralarsson/video/7612083771013385475). “Thank you so much for having me, this was a big moment for me!!!”
