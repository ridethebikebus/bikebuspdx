About Us

Bike Bus PDX, a fiscally-sponsored project of Cascadia Action, helps school PTAs organize bike buses throughout the Portland Metro area — students riding set bike routes to school together. We are working towards a Portland where children are biking and valued with infrastructure, funding, education and access for all! Over 25 Bike Buses exist across Portland, the largest concentration in North America, and have replaced roughly 250,000 car trips over three years.
